Grison Jump

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Grison Jump ("the app", "we") is a free mobile arcade game for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. We respect your privacy.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information ourselves. The app has no user accounts, and we do not operate any servers or databases. Game progress, settings and in-game items are stored only locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process certain data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, and information about how you interact with ads. This data is collected directly by Google and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data and have no access to it.

Consent and your choices

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where required will see a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) when they first open the app. You can accept or decline personalised advertising, and you can change your choice at any time via the privacy options in the app settings, where available.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights (including access, deletion and objection under GDPR) through your Google account settings or by contacting Google.

Children's privacy

Grison Jump is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help resolve the issue.

Security

Since all game data stays on your device, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping your device and the app up to date.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date shown above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at grison.lore.app@gmail.com.
